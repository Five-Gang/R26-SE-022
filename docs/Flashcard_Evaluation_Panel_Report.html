📐 Academic Justification: The Novel ERS Metric

Formula and weightings designed for Research Contribution RC4, establishing a verifiable educational index.

ERS = 0.40 × LOCS + 0.30 × BAS + 0.20 × SGR + 0.10 × Coherence

LOCS (40%): Learning Outcome Coverage Score across target syllabus.
BAS (30%): Bloom's Alignment Score comparing cognitive depth to target LO verb.
SGR (20%): Source Grounding Rate checking verifiable slide provenance.
Coherence (10%): Absence of malformed headers or numbering artifacts.
